<section class="project-case" id="50x2030">
  <div class="project-case__header">
    <p class="eyebrow">Multi-partner initiative · Agricultural surveys</p>
    <h2>50x2030 Initiative</h2>
    <p class="project-case__lead">Strengthening integrated agricultural survey systems and the evidence base for agricultural development and SDG monitoring.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="project-case__details">
    <div><h3>The challenge</h3><p>Many statistical systems need efficient, sustainable approaches to producing regular agricultural and rural statistics while managing limited resources and changing information needs.</p></div>
    <div><h3>Areas of contribution</h3><p>My related methodological work covers agricultural survey approaches, sampling guidance, data collection design and indicator production. The initiative is multi-partner; this page does not claim individual ownership of collective results.</p></div>
    <div><h3>Selected outputs</h3><ul><li>Research on integrated survey design and the measurement of agricultural indicators.</li><li>Technical guidance on sampling and survey preparation.</li><li>Lessons on survey design, quality and implementation.</li></ul></div>
  </div>
