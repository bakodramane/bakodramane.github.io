<p class="page-lede">AI for Official Statistics is a bilingual personal knowledge hub by Dramane Bako, focusing on evidence, practical methodological considerations and responsible artificial intelligence.</p>

The blog follows credible developments in AI and their implications for survey and census programmes, administrative data, editing, imputation, quality assurance, statistical analysis and dissemination.

Its purpose is to help statisticians, researchers and national statistical offices navigate emerging tools without neglecting methodological rigour, reproducibility, privacy or public trust.

The articles and resources are independent personal commentary and do not represent FAO or any other institution.
