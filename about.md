<p class="page-lede">I am Dramane Bako, a statistician, data scientist and researcher working on the modernisation of official statistics, with a particular focus on agricultural data systems, survey methodology and responsible AI.</p>

<div class="profile-intro">
  <div>
    <p class="eyebrow">Professional profile</p>
    <h2>Bringing methodological rigour and practical innovation together</h2>
    <p>My professional work includes contributions to FAO agricultural statistics programmes, international methodological guidance, survey sampling and integrated agricultural surveys. I am interested in how national statistical offices and development partners can improve the quality, availability and practical use of statistical information.</p>
    <p>Alongside methodological work, I develop and study applications of data science, machine learning and AI that support survey operations, data quality assurance, analytical workflows and statistical dissemination.</p>
    <p>This personal website brings together selected publications, professional projects, open-source tools and independent commentary. Views are personal and do not represent FAO or any other institution.</p>
